FAQ

Common questions

Do guests need to download an app?

No — the menu opens instantly in any phone browser after a QR scan. Nothing to install.

Can I change prices in real time?

Yes. Any edit in the Menu Builder is live on every guest's menu within seconds.

Does NEXORA support multiple branches?

Pro and above support multiple branches, each with its own hours, contact details, and QR code.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, all plans are month-to-month with no long-term contract.
